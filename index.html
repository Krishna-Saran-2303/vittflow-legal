Transparent Data Usage & Google Drive Integration

VittFlow requests access to the Google Drive appDataFolder scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata). This integration is central to our "Privacy-First" mission:

  • Decentralized Storage: We operate without a central backend. All user records reside exclusively on the user's device and their private Google Drive.
  • Encrypted Synchronization: The application facilitates the creation of a secure, AES-256-GCM encrypted backup file (vittflow_backup.enc) within a hidden application folder.
  • End-to-End Security: Encryption occurs locally on the Android device. Developers and Google are physically unable to access raw financial data.
  • User Sovereignty: Users retain full control. Access can be revoked instantly through Google Account security settings, and backups can only be decrypted with the user's unique identity or recovery key.
