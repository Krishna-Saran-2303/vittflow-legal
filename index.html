Application Purpose & Description

VittFlow is designed to solve the problem of insecure and fragmented personal finance tracking. Our mission is to provide a 100% private, offline-first platform where users can manage their entire financial life without relying on third-party servers.

Specifically, the application provides:

  • Local Financial Ledger: Securely log income, expenses, and savings in a local Room database.
  • Investment Monitoring: Track SIP plans and logs with automated step-up calculations.
  • Privacy-First Backup: We use the Google Drive appDataFolder scope to allow users to store their own encrypted backup files. This ensures that only the user can access their data, and VittFlow developers never see their raw financial records.
