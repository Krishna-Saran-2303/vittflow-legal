App Functionality & Purpose

VittFlow is a comprehensive personal finance architect for Android, designed to give users absolute control over their financial data. It serves as a private digital ledger for managing wealth, planning for the future, and monitoring investments in a secure, server-less environment.

Financial Tracking

Complete management of income categories, monthly earnings, and savings transactions with real-time balance calculations.

Investment Portfolio

Advanced tracking for Systematic Investment Plans (SIP), including annual step-up cycles and automated payment logs.

Smart Planning

Tools to build and track an Emergency Fund and specialized Financial Goals (like travel or major purchases) with intelligent contribution logging.

Transparency: How We Use Your Data

VittFlow requests access to the Google Drive appDataFolder scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata) for one specific purpose: Privacy-First User Backups.

  • No Central Server: We do not store your financial records on our own servers. Your data is stored locally in an encrypted Room database.
  • Encrypted Synchronization: To allow data recovery across devices, the app creates an encrypted backup file (vittflow_backup.enc) in a hidden folder on your personal Google Drive.
  • End-to-End Security: Data is encrypted using AES-256-GCM on your device BEFORE upload. Neither Google nor the app developers can read your raw financial records.
  • Limited Access: We cannot see or access your personal photos, documents, or any other files in your Drive.
