Detailed Purpose of Google Drive Access

To ensure 100% user privacy, VittFlow does not use a central server to store your financial data. Instead, we use the Google Drive appDataFolder scope.

This allows you to create a secure, private backup of your local database. Our application only has permission to access a hidden folder that is isolated from your personal files. We use this access exclusively to:

  • Encrypt & Upload: Save your local financial records as an encrypted file (vittflow_backup.enc).
  • Recover Data: Restore your records if you switch devices or reinstall the application.

Note: VittFlow developers have no access to your files or your raw financial data. Everything is end-to-end encrypted.
