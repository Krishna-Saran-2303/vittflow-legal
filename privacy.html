Privacy Policy for VittFlow

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

1. Data Collection

VittFlow stores all financial data locally on your device. We do not collect or store your data on our servers.

2. Google Drive Backup

VittFlow uses the drive.appdata scope to store encrypted backups. We only access the hidden application folder. We cannot read your personal files or photos.

3. Encryption

Backups are encrypted using AES-256-GCM locally. Only you have the key (via your account identity or recovery password).

4. Third-Party Services

We use Google Sign-In for authentication. Their privacy policies apply to account data.
