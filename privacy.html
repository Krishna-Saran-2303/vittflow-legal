Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how VittFlow ("we", "our", or "the Application") handles your personal and financial information. Our core philosophy is "Privacy by Design", ensuring that users have complete ownership of their data.

1. Data Collection and Usage

VittFlow is an offline-first application. We do not operate a central server or backend database. Your data is handled as follows:

  • Financial Records: All entries regarding income, savings, SIPs, and goals are stored exclusively in a local Room database on your Android device.
  • Identity Information: When you use Google Sign-In, we store only your email address and display name locally to facilitate the cloud backup feature.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

2. Google Drive "appDataFolder" Access

To provide a secure data recovery mechanism, VittFlow utilizes the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope. This allows the application to:

  • Store a single encrypted backup file (vittflow_backup.enc) in a hidden, isolated folder on your Google Drive.
  • The Application cannot access, view, or modify any other files, photos, or documents in your personal Google Drive.
  • Users have the absolute right to delete these backups at any time through the Application or Google Account settings.

3. Encryption Standards

Data security is our highest priority. Before any synchronization with Google Drive occurs, your data is encrypted locally using the AES-256-GCM (Advanced Encryption Standard with Galois/Counter Mode) authenticated encryption algorithm.

The encryption key is managed by the user. If you lose your Recovery Password, the VittFlow team has no technical means of decrypting or recovering your backup.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data only for as long as the Application is installed on your device or the backup exists in your Google Drive. You may delete all local data by clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app. Cloud backups can be deleted via the Google Drive interface or the "Sign Out" process in the app.

5. Children's Privacy

VittFlow is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
