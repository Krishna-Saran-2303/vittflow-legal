Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

1. Data Collection & Usage

VittFlow is an offline-first application. All financial records you enter (income, savings, SIPs, goals) are stored exclusively on your device. We do not transmit this data to our own servers.

Google Account: We use Google Sign-In to identify your account for cloud backup features. Your email and name are stored only locally.

2. Google Drive Access (Sensitive Scope)

VittFlow requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope to provide a backup feature. This allows the app to:

  • Create an encrypted backup file in a hidden folder on your Google Drive.
  • Retrieve only the files created by VittFlow.

We cannot see, access, or modify any other files in your personal Google Drive.

3. Encryption & Security

All data uploaded to Google Drive is encrypted locally on your device using AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is derived from your identity or a user-provided Recovery Password. Neither Google nor the VittFlow developers can read your raw data.
