Privacy Policy for VittFlow

Effective Date: July 26, 2026

1. Introduction

VittFlow ("we") is committed to protecting your financial privacy. Our application is built on the principle of Local-First Storage, meaning your sensitive data stays on your device.

2. Google Drive Data Access

VittFlow requests access to your Google Drive via the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope. This is a restricted, hidden folder created specifically for our app.

  • Usage: We only use this access to store one file: vittflow_backup.enc.
  • Limitation: We cannot see your photos, documents, or any other files in your personal Drive.
  • Security: All data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM locally on your Android device before it is ever uploaded.

3. Data Deletion

If you choose to delete your data, you can do so within the app settings. Revoking Google Drive access through your Google Account settings will also immediately stop all cloud synchronization.
