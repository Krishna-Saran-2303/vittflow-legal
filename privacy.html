Privacy Policy for VittFlow

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

At VittFlow, we believe that your financial history is your business alone. This policy details how our application handles your data with a strict privacy-first approach.

1. What Data We Back Up

If you choose to use the Google Drive Backup feature, the following records from your local database are encrypted and synchronized:

  • User Settings & Profile (username, ratios, currency)
  • Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) & Payment Logs
  • Income Categories & Monthly Entries
  • Savings Transactions & Emergency Fund progress
  • Financial Goals & Contribution History
  • Yearly Profit/Loss Records

2. Google Drive "appDataFolder" Scope

VittFlow requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata permission. This is a restricted scope that only allows the app to see the files it has created itself.

  • Security: We cannot see your photos, personal documents, or emails.
  • Encryption: All data is encrypted locally using AES-256-GCM (Authenticated Encryption) BEFORE upload.
  • Control: You can revoke this access at any time through your Google Account settings.

3. Data Ownership

VittFlow is a server-less application. We do not have a backend database. Your data exists only in two places: your local phone memory and your personal Google Drive account. We do not sell, share, or analyze your data.

4. Your Responsibilities

Users are responsible for setting a secure **Recovery Password** within the app. Because we use end-to-end encryption, the VittFlow team cannot recover your data if you lose this password.
